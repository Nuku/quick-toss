# Dice sound credits

`dice-hits.mp3` is the audio sprite distributed by [Dicer](https://github.com/roileaf/dicer). It contains individual dice hits cut from [SFX Pack – Rolling Dice](https://halfwitsfailedcrits.itch.io/sfx-pack-rolling-dice) by Halfwits & Failed Crits, recorded by Jonathan Swenson and released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
